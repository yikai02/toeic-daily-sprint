⚡ 秒殺錨點：Part 7 星號小字與但書條件 (*Footnotes) 獵殺雷達
  • 🔹 👀 看到文本底部附帶「*Notice / *Note / *Terms」➔ 題目必定至少考一題細節陷阱！
  • 🔹 👀 注意單複數名詞變化：criterion (單數) vs. criteria (複數) ➔ 動詞一致性秒殺！
  • 🏆 法則：⚡ 教練金句：正文快速瀏覽抓架構，星號但書慢讀抓正解！
📐 文法模組 #1：Signals 3：關係代名詞所有格陷阱（whose + N vs. of which + the + N）
  • 📐 秒殺公式：✅ 先行詞 + whose + 無冠詞名詞 ｜ ✅ 先行詞, of which + the + 名詞
  • 💡 觀念解析：關係代名詞所有格 whose 本身已取代所有格與冠詞，因此 whose 後方的名詞前「絕對不能有定冠詞 the」。反之，若題目空格後方的名詞前已經印出定冠詞「the + 名詞」，則絕對不可選 whose，必須改選介系詞片語「of which」（構成 of which the + N）。
  • ✅ 正確示範：Remedica, of which the key pharmaceutical products have generated enormous revenues, will expand overseas next quarter. （主打藥品創造龐大營收的 Remedica 公司將於下季拓展海外市場。）
  • ⚠️ 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：看到所有格語意就直接選 whose，卻漏看空格後面已經印有定冠詞 the（whose the key products 為嚴重文法錯誤）。
📐 文法模組 #2：Signals 4：單複數複合名詞陷阱（All / Several + 單數名詞修飾語 + 複數核心名詞）
  • 📐 秒殺公式：✅ All / Several / Numerous + [單數名詞修飾語] + [複數可數名詞主詞] + 動詞
  • 💡 觀念解析：量詞 All、Several、Many、Numerous 修飾可數名詞時，核心名詞必須為複數。若空格前方已印有一個單數可數名詞（如 photo、 safety、 customer），代表該單數名詞只是「形容詞化名詞修飾語（Noun Adjunct）」，空格本身才是真正的主詞中心語，必填複數名詞！
  • ✅ 正確示範：All photo submissions received after the Friday deadline will be automatically returned to the senders. （所有在週五截止期限後收到的攝影投件作品都將自動退回給寄件人。）
  • ⚠️ 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤以為前方的 photo 已經是主詞而選動詞或單數名詞 submission（All photo 語法不成立，必須是 All photo submissions）。

PRODUCT LINE: Volta Chef Master High-Speed Blender (Model X-200)

1. Reason for Voluntary Recall
Volta Electric, in voluntary cooperation with consumer safety regulators, is recalling all Model X-200 high-speed blenders manufactured between March and September 2026. Internal laboratory diagnostics revealed that an internal wiring defect may cause the motor to overheat during extended usage, presenting a potential fire hazard. Consumers are urged to immediately discontinue using the appliance and unplug it from electrical outlets.

2. Refund and Replacement Remedies
Consumers are entitled to a full cash refund of $149 or a free replacement unit featuring our upgraded brushless motor. To receive a full refund or free replacement, customers must present the original purchase receipt or the serial barcode label affixed to the underside of the blender base.

*Important Program Deadline: All recall claims accompanied by proper documentation must be processed before December 31, 2026. Claims filed after December 31 will be eligible only for store credit redeemable on Volta kitchen appliances. Prepaid shipping return boxes can be ordered at www.voltaelectric.com/recall.
