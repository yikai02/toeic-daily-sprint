PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：Phase 2 大驗收：高頻名詞/形容詞秒殺錨點
  • • 👀 看到 in ________ with ➔ 100% 填入名詞「compliance」（in compliance with）！
  • • 👀 看到 is strictly ________ ➔ 填入形容詞「mandatory」（強制的）！
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：合規遵從選 compliance，強制必須選 mandatory！
文法模組 #1：Rule 3：簡化關係子句（主句已有真動詞，無關代時必選分詞）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ 主詞 + [過去分詞 p.p. / 現在分詞 V-ing 後位修飾片語] + 主句主要真動詞
  • 觀念解析：解題時務必先掃描整句找出「主句真動詞」（如 will be retained）。若主詞後方沒有關係代名詞（which / that / who），則主詞與主句真動詞之間的空格只能填入「分詞（V-ing 表主動 / p.p. 表被動）」，絕不可再填入 are left 或 have been left 等真動詞！
  • 正確示範：Personal items left behind by passengers on commuter trains will be retained at the central lost-and-found office for 30 days. （乘客遺留在通勤列車上的個人物品將於中央失物招領處保留三十天。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：沒看到句尾的真動詞 will be retained，誤將空格當作主句動詞而選 are left behind 或 have been left behind。
文法模組 #2：Rule 1：疑問詞引導不定詞 (how best to) 與 介系詞關係代名詞 (介系詞 + whom / which)
  • 秒殺公式：✅ S + V + [疑問副詞 how / where / when] + best to + V ｜ ✅ 先行詞 (人), 介系詞 + whom + S + V
  • 觀念解析：多益 Part 5 兩大結構秒殺：(1) 疑問詞搭配不定詞片語作受詞（disagree over how best to advertise... 如何最妥善地宣傳），當及物動詞後已有名詞受詞時，絕不可選代名詞 what，必選副詞 how；(2) 介系詞後若接指人的先行詞，必用關係代名詞受格 whom（如 without whom, to whom, with whom），選 them 會造成 Comma Splice（逗號連接兩獨立子句）之嚴重文法錯誤，且 that 前絕不可有介系詞！
  • 正確示範：The board debated over how best to allocate the emergency contingency reserve, without which the overseas branch could not operate. （董事會激辯如何最妥善地分配緊急準備金，若沒有這筆資金，海外分行將無法運作。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 what（what to do 只有在 do 後面缺受詞時才成立；advertise 後面已有名詞受詞，必須用 how）；先行詞為人時誤選 without that（關代 that 前方嚴禁介系詞與逗號）。

SAFETY COMPLIANCE CIRCULAR: ZC-HSE-2026-05
ISSUED BY: Environmental Health & Safety Division, Zenith Chemicals Inc.
EFFECTIVE DATE: November 8, 2026
MANDATORY FOR: All Laboratory Technicians, Production Chemists, and Plant Supervisors

1. Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Zenith Chemicals maintains uncompromising zero-tolerance safety standards regarding hazardous workplace chemical exposure. When handling volatile organic solvents, corrosive acids, or pressurized catalysts in Synthesis Hall B, laboratory personnel are required to wear chemical-resistant aprons and eye goggles at all times. Standard cotton lab coats and prescription spectacles do not meet safety compliance specifications and are strictly prohibited in active chemical handling zones.

2. Emergency Decontamination Stations & Eyewashes
In accordance with international occupational safety regulations and chemical hazard containment directives, emergency wash stations and high-flow eyewash units are situated in every corridor throughout the chemical processing facility. These life-safety stations undergo automated pressure testing every Monday morning by facility engineers.

*Accidental Exposure Protocol: In the event of skin contact or chemical spillage, affected staff must vacate the contaminated area immediately and rinse the exposed body part for a minimum of fifteen minutes, unless medically advised otherwise by emergency health responders. Unreported exposure incidents will result in formal disciplinary review.
