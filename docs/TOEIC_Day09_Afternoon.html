📐 PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合/展開 ▲
⚡ 秒殺錨點：不定代名詞秒殺：看後面有沒有名詞，以及是單數還是複數？
  • 🔹 👀 空格後有【單數名詞】➔ 選 another（another branch 另一家分行）
  • 🔹 👀 空格後有【複數名詞】➔ 選 other（other branches 其他分行）
  • 🏆 法則：⚡ 教練金句：後有單數選 another，後有複數選 other，後無名詞選 others！
📐 文法模組 #1：Rule 2：條件連接詞（provided that / as long as）vs. 範圍界定詞（as far as）
  • 📐 秒殺公式：✅ provided that / providing that / as long as + S + V（只要……／在……條件下） ｜ ✅ as far as + S + know / be concerned（就……範圍而言）
  • 💡 觀念解析：多益常將 provided that / as long as（表條件：只要……）與 as far as（表範圍程度：就……而言）放在同一組選項中。若句意為「只要滿足某項前提條件，即可執行某事」，必選 provided that 或 as long as；若句尾為 is concerned 或 I know，才選 as far as。
  • ✅ 正確示範：Full tuition reimbursement can be approved provided that both the department supervisor and human resources sign the authorization form. （只要部門主管與人資部雙方皆簽署授權表單，即可核准全額學費補助。）
  • ⚠️ 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：中文都翻成「只要／就……」，誤用 as far as 引導條件子句。
📐 文法模組 #2：Signals 1：be critical of + N vs. criticize + O（形容詞與及物動詞之介系詞訊號）
  • 📐 秒殺公式：✅ be + critical + of + 名詞受詞 ｜ ✅ criticize + 名詞受詞（絕不可加 of）
  • 💡 觀念解析：看到空格位於 be 動詞（was / were / remain）之後，且空格後方緊接介系詞 of 時，必須秒選形容詞 critical（對……持批評態度的）。反之，若空格為主要動詞且後方直接接名詞受詞（無介系詞 of），才選及物動詞 criticize。
  • ✅ 正確示範：The head of marketing was openly critical of the board's decision to reduce digital advertising expenditure. （行銷主管公開對董事會削減數位廣告支出的決定持批評態度。）
  • ⚠️ 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 was criticized of（criticize 為及物動詞，被動語態應為 was criticized by/for，主動亦不可加 of）。

Metropolitan Transportation Council — Bridge Closure and Commuter Detour Guidelines
CIVIC INFRASTRUCTURE ADVISORY: MTC-TRAF-2026-44
ISSUING AGENCY: Metropolitan Transportation Council & Department of Public Works
DATE: November 12, 2026
IMPACT ZONE: West Harbor Crossing Corridor

1. Scheduled Weekend Bridge Closure
The Metropolitan Transportation Council announces that the West Harbor Suspension Bridge will undergo a complete vehicular and cycling closure this weekend, commencing Friday at 10:00 PM and concluding Monday at 5:00 AM. This closure is necessary to install seismic reinforcement braces and repair structural joint dampeners, following our triennial seismic safety assessment.

2. Alternative Routing & Tunnel Toll Waiver
Motorists traveling between downtown and the western districts are advised to utilize the Midtown Tunnel as the primary detour route. To alleviate traffic congestion and expedite weekend journeys, toll fees will be temporarily exempted at all Midtown Tunnel toll plazas for the duration of the bridge closure.

*Public Transit & Pedestrian Shuttle Services: Harbor ferry services will operate on an expanded weekend schedule with boats departing every twenty minutes. Furthermore, cross-bay pedestrian shuttle buses will run every fifteen minutes between the north and south transit centers. Detailed detour diagrams and real-time travel alerts are accessible at www.metrotransit.gov/harbor-repair.
