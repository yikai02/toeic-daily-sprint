PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：Phase 1 四大陷阱終極合體雷達
  • • 1️⃣ 看到空格後無動詞 ➔ 介系詞（despite, in spite of, owing to, prior to）
  • • 2️⃣ 看到 while/once 帶受詞 ➔ 主動 -ing，被動 -ed
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：秒殺題靠位置，語意題靠語塊搭配！
文法模組 #1：Rule 1：疑問詞引導不定詞 (how best to) 與 介系詞關係代名詞 (介系詞 + whom / which)
  • 秒殺公式：✅ S + V + [疑問副詞 how / where / when] + best to + V ｜ ✅ 先行詞 (人), 介系詞 + whom + S + V
  • 觀念解析：多益 Part 5 兩大結構秒殺：(1) 疑問詞搭配不定詞片語作受詞（disagree over how best to advertise... 如何最妥善地宣傳），當及物動詞後已有名詞受詞時，絕不可選代名詞 what，必選副詞 how；(2) 介系詞後若接指人的先行詞，必用關係代名詞受格 whom（如 without whom, to whom, with whom），選 them 會造成 Comma Splice（逗號連接兩獨立子句）之嚴重文法錯誤，且 that 前絕不可有介系詞！
  • 正確示範：The board debated over how best to allocate the emergency contingency reserve, without which the overseas branch could not operate. （董事會激辯如何最妥善地分配緊急準備金，若沒有這筆資金，海外分行將無法運作。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 what（what to do 只有在 do 後面缺受詞時才成立；advertise 後面已有名詞受詞，必須用 how）；先行詞為人時誤選 without that（關代 that 前方嚴禁介系詞與逗號）。
文法模組 #2：Signals 1：be critical of + N vs. criticize + O（形容詞與及物動詞之介系詞訊號）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ be + critical + of + 名詞受詞 ｜ ✅ criticize + 名詞受詞（絕不可加 of）
  • 觀念解析：看到空格位於 be 動詞（was / were / remain）之後，且空格後方緊接介系詞 of 時，必須秒選形容詞 critical（對……持批評態度的）。反之，若空格為主要動詞且後方直接接名詞受詞（無介系詞 of），才選及物動詞 criticize。
  • 正確示範：The head of marketing was openly critical of the board's decision to reduce digital advertising expenditure. （行銷主管公開對董事會削減數位廣告支出的決定持批評態度。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 was criticized of（criticize 為及物動詞，被動語態應為 was criticized by/for，主動亦不可加 of）。

Section 2. Aggregate Coverage Limits and Insured Perils:
Veritas Underwriters provides comprehensive property loss protection for all manufacturing plants, research cleanrooms, and executive office facilities situated within the Crestline Technology Campus. The policy provides a total aggregate insurance coverage of $5 million against structural fire, water main rupture, windstorm damage, and verified industrial equipment collapse occurring on insured corporate premises during the policy period, with supplemental debris removal indemnification.

Section 6. Fire Suppression System Maintenance Covenants:
To keep policy terms fully operational, the insured must adhere to strict fire safety protocols. Sprinkler valves, backflow preventers, and pressure gauges must receive servicing quarterly (every three months) by state-certified fire protection contractors, with dated inspection tags affixed to each main manifold.

*Exclusion Clause: Coverage is nullified in instances of gross negligence. The policy expressly excludes damage resulting from uncertified electrical rewiring, unauthorized panel modifications, or non-compliant machinery installations performed without certified municipal electrical permits.
