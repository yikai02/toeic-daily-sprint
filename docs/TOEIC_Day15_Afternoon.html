PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：商務除外介系詞 (Exclusionary Prepositions) 視覺秒殺錨點
  • • 👀 空格後為名詞片語 [________ any unexpected delays] ➔ 秒選介系詞「Barring」或「Without」！
  • • 👀 看到 [________ for 名詞] ➔ 秒選「Except for」；看到 [________ from 名詞] ➔ 秒選「Aside from」！
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：看到 for 配 except，看到 from 配 aside，看到純名詞選 barring！
文法模組 #1：Rule 2：條件連接詞（provided that / as long as）vs. 範圍界定詞（as far as）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ provided that / providing that / as long as + S + V（只要……／在……條件下） ｜ ✅ as far as + S + know / be concerned（就……範圍而言）
  • 觀念解析：多益常將 provided that / as long as（表條件：只要……）與 as far as（表範圍程度：就……而言）放在同一組選項中。若句意為「只要滿足某項前提條件，即可執行某事」，必選 provided that 或 as long as；若句尾為 is concerned 或 I know，才選 as far as。
  • 正確示範：Full tuition reimbursement can be approved provided that both the department supervisor and human resources sign the authorization form. （只要部門主管與人資部雙方皆簽署授權表單，即可核准全額學費補助。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：中文都翻成「只要／就……」，誤用 as far as 引導條件子句。
文法模組 #2：Rule 2：分詞形容詞 (varied selection / informed decision) 與 否定遞進 (let alone)
  • 秒殺公式：✅ a + varied + selection / assortment + of... ｜ ✅ make + an + informed + decision ｜ ✅ 否定句 / lack + N, + let alone + 更高階事物
  • 觀念解析：多益常考「形近詞與固定搭配」盲點：(1) 修飾單數集合名詞 selection 需用分詞形容詞 varied（多樣化的），various 則直接修飾複數名詞（various options）；(2) make an informed decision 指做出深思熟慮的明智決定，informative 則是修飾演講或書籍提供豐富資訊；(3) let alone 專門置於否定或缺乏（lack / cannot）句境後，表「更不用說、何況」；(4) be opposed to + N / V-ing（to 為介系詞），主動 oppose 則直接接受詞。
  • 正確示範：Bar Italia offers a varied selection of gourmet teas, allowing clients to make an informed choice, let alone enjoying complimentary pastries. （義大利餐吧提供多樣化的頂級茶品選品，讓顧客能做出明智抉擇，更不用說還能享受免費糕點。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：受中文影響說 a various selection（文法錯誤）；誤選 rather than 或 in contrast 替代 let alone。

Corporate Finance Directive — Standard Operating Procedure: Travel Expense Reimbursements
FINANCE DIRECTIVE: FIN-SOP-2026-04
APPLIES TO: All Global Staff, Field Consultants, and Department Heads
ISSUED BY: Corporate Accounting & Financial Audit Division
EFFECTIVE: November 5, 2026

1. Purpose and Submission Timelines
To standardize international and domestic business travel accounting, the Finance Directorate has established updated expense filing regulations. All employees traveling on company business must file their formal expense reimbursement claims within ten business days following trip conclusion. Late submissions will experience processing delays and may be deferred to the subsequent fiscal quarter.

2. Daily Per Diem Thresholds and Exclusions
The standard business travel per diem provides up to $75 per day for meals and personal incidentals while on official travel status. If a daily meal expense surpasses $75 without written consent from a division vice president, the excess amount is deducted from the next paycheck. Alcohol, minibar purchases, and personal laundry services remain strictly non-reimbursable.

*Mandatory Receipt Requirements: Itemized receipts showing payment method and date are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $25. As a minor administrative exemption, small incidentals, such as a taxi fare receipt totaling $18 or parking meter stubs under $20, do not require itemized receipts; standard merchant credit card receipts or terminal printouts are acceptable.
