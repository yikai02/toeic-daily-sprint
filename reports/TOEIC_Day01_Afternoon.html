🧠 早間記憶快篩 (6 題)
1. Ms. Lin was appointed to ________ [監督] the infrastructure project.
💡 線索：動詞，字首為 o，監督主管
🛡️ 點擊查看第 1 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：oversee (oversee the project)
2. Due to an administrative ________ [疏失], the invoice was sent to the wrong vendor.
💡 線索：名詞，字首為 o，不可與 oversee 混淆
🛡️ 點擊查看第 2 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：oversight (an unfortunate oversight)
3. Staff members must meet tenure requirements to be eligible ________ [介系詞] the annual bonus.
💡 線索：符合……資格
🛡️ 點擊查看第 3 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：for (eligible for)
4. Customers are entitled ________ [介系詞] a full replacement for defective items.
💡 線索：有權獲得……
🛡️ 點擊查看第 4 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：to (entitled to)
5. VIP ticket holders are exempted ________ [介系詞] paying baggage surcharges.
💡 線索：被免除……費用
🛡️ 點擊查看第 5 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：from (exempted from)
6. Please ensure that the newly purchased scanner is compatible ________ [介系詞] our network.
💡 線索：與……相容
🛡️ 點擊查看第 6 題正解 揭曉
👉 正解：with (compatible with)
🪤 間隔重複錯題變體 (8 題)
陷阱題 1 🔥 易錯陷阱
The auditor noted that the accounting discrepancy was a minor ________ rather than intentional fraud.
(A) oversee
(B) oversight
(C) overseer
(D) overseeing
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 1 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：名詞詞性與意義辨析：a minor + [名詞]。overseer 是監工（人），oversight 才是疏失（事物）。
💡 解析：句中對比 'rather than intentional fraud'（而非蓄意詐欺），空格需填入指『無心疏失』的名詞，故選 (B) oversight。
陷阱題 2 🔥 易錯陷阱
The proposed software upgrade is not fully ________ with older office workstations.
(A) proficient
(B) compatible
(C) vulnerable
(D) exempted
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 2 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：介系詞搭配陷阱：with 出現，秒選 compatible。
💡 解析：compatible with（與……相容）。proficient 接 in；vulnerable 接 to；exempted 接 from。
陷阱題 3 🔥 易錯陷阱
________ Mr. Henderson worked through the night, he was unable to finalize the client proposal before the morning deadline.
(A) Despite
(B) Although
(C) In spite of
(D) Owing to
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 3 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：看空格後有沒有動詞！後方有完整動詞 'worked'，絕不能選介系詞 (A), (C), (D)！
💡 解析：空格後為 'Mr. Henderson worked through the night'（主詞 + 動詞 worked），句意表轉折讓步，秒選從屬連接詞 (B) Although！
陷阱題 4 🔥 易錯陷阱
The lead mechanical engineer will ________ inspect the turbine before authorizing operational testing.
(A) thorough
(B) thoroughly
(C) thoroughness
(D) through
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 4 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：will ________ inspect（助動詞與動詞原形之間的夾心餅乾，塞入副詞 -ly）！
💡 解析：修飾動詞 inspect，需用副詞 (B) thoroughly（徹底地 / 仔細地）。
陷阱題 5 🔥 易錯陷阱
In the event ________ an emergency power failure, the backup generators will activate automatically.
(A) that
(B) of
(C) as
(D) for
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 5 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：後面是純名詞片語 'an emergency power failure'，無動詞！
💡 解析：純名詞片語選介系詞片語 in the event of。若有 S+V 句子才選 in the event that。
陷阱題 6 🔥 易錯陷阱
All research assistants must adhere strictly ________ established biohazard containment protocols.
(A) to
(B) with
(C) in
(D) for
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 6 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(A)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：adhere 永遠搭配 to（遵守）！不可誤選 with。
💡 解析：adhere strictly to... 為商務英文最高頻必考片語，故選 (A)。
陷阱題 7 🔥 易錯陷阱
While ________ the quarterly budget sheets, the controller discovered an unaccounted deficit.
(A) examine
(B) examining
(C) examined
(D) examination
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 7 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：分詞構句減化：主詞 主動 檢視報表並帶出受詞 'the budget sheets'！
💡 解析：主動做動作且帶受詞，while 後方減化分詞選現在分詞 (B) examining。
陷阱題 8 🔥 易錯陷阱
Contractual guidelines require departing commercial tenants to ________ the office suites by month-end.
(A) vacate
(B) vacant
(C) vacantly
(D) vacancy
🛡️ 點擊查看陷阱題 8 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(A)
⚠️ 命題陷阱點：to + [動詞原形] + the office suites！
💡 解析：不定詞後接動詞原形，vacate（騰空/搬出物業）。vacant 為形容詞；vacancy 為名詞職缺。
📑 Part 7 細節抓漏微閱讀 (6 題)
📄 Internal Policy Notice (文本 1)
Annual Health & Wellness Subsidy Guidelines
All permanent employees who have completed at least six months of service under the direction of a certified team lead are eligible for an annual health subsidy of up to $500. This fund can be applied toward gym memberships or ergonomically approved home-office furniture.

*Note: Part-time staff and temporary interns are exempted from the mandatory activity log but are entitled to a modified subsidy of $150, provided that their application is submitted prior to November 15.
Part 7 題 1
What is required for temporary interns to receive a subsidy?
(A) Complete six months of full-time service.
(B) Maintain a daily workout log.
(C) Apply before November 15.
(D) Purchase certified gym equipment.
🔍 點擊查看題 1 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(C)
🔍 防漏看雷達：看到 *Note 星號附註中的條件句 'provided that their application is submitted prior to November 15'！實習生不必滿 6 個月，但必須在 11/15 前提出申請。
💡 詳解：文中星號標記明確指出實習生享有修改後的補助，前提是申請在 11 月 15 日前提出（prior to November 15 = before November 15），故選 (C)。
Part 7 題 2
What is TRUE about permanent employees regarding the subsidy?
(A) They must have at least six months of continuous tenure.
(B) They can claim up to $1,000 annually.
(C) They are exempted from submitting wellness receipts.
(D) They must purchase their own office laptops.
🔍 點擊查看題 2 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(A)
🔍 防漏看雷達：首句開頭細節 'completed at least six months of service'！
💡 詳解：第一句提及 'All permanent employees who have completed at least six months of service... are eligible'，與 (A) 意思完全相符。
Part 7 題 3
Which item is eligible for reimbursement under this wellness policy?
(A) Standard home office stationery supplies.
(B) Ergonomically certified home-office furniture.
(C) Monthly public transit commuting cards.
(D) Uncertified personal recreational items.
🔍 點擊查看題 3 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
🔍 防漏看雷達：內文具體名詞替換！'applied toward gym memberships or ergonomically approved home-office furniture' ＝ 'Ergonomically certified home-office furniture'！
💡 詳解：文中第一段第二句清楚列出補助可用於健身房會籍或人體工學核准家具，故選 (B)。
📄 Corporate Travel & Attire Memo (文本 2)
Summit Logistics: Dress Code and Baggage Allowance
To: Regional Sales Representatives
From: Events Planning Directorate
Date: November 4

All representatives attending the annual Chicago summit will be provided accommodation at the Grand Plaza. Standard business attire is mandatory for daytime panel sessions. However, casual garments are permitted exclusively during the Friday evening networking reception.

*Luggage Allowance: The company will reimburse luggage fees for up to two checked bags, provided that luggage receipts are submitted within 7 days of trip conclusion. Claims submitted after this 7-day threshold will be rejected without exception.
Part 7 題 4
What type of clothing is permitted exclusively on Friday evening?
(A) Formal evening tuxedos and gowns.
(B) Casual clothing.
(C) Branded sports jerseys.
(D) Traditional ethnic costumes.
🔍 點擊查看題 4 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
🔍 防漏看雷達：限制副詞 exclusively！'casual garments are permitted exclusively during the Friday evening networking reception'！garments ＝ clothing！
💡 詳解：文中指出週五晚間社交酒會唯一允許休閒便服（casual garments = casual clothing），故選 (B)。
Part 7 題 5
How many checked bags can delegates have reimbursed?
(A) One bag only.
(B) Up to two checked bags.
(C) Three checked bags.
(D) Unlimited bags with manager approval.
🔍 點擊查看題 5 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
🔍 防漏看雷達：具體數字細節 'reimburse luggage fees for up to two checked bags'！
💡 詳解：行李補助條款明確載明可報銷至多兩件托運行李（up to two checked bags），故選 (B)。
Part 7 題 6
Under what condition will a luggage reimbursement claim be denied?
(A) If the representative flew on an unapproved airline.
(B) If receipts are submitted more than 7 days after the trip ends.
(C) If the delegate traveled alone without a colleague.
(D) If checked bags weighed over 20 kilograms each.
🔍 點擊查看題 6 答案與防漏看雷達 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
🔍 防漏看雷達：星號附註最後一句 'Claims submitted after this 7-day threshold will be rejected without exception'！rejected ＝ denied！
💡 詳解：文末星號強調若超過 7 天期限繳交收據，報銷申請將毫無例外地遭到拒絕（rejected = denied），故選 (B)。
