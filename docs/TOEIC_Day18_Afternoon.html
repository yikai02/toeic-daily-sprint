PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：Phase 2 大驗收：高頻名詞/形容詞秒殺錨點
  • • 👀 看到 in ________ with ➔ 100% 填入名詞「compliance」（in compliance with）！
  • • 👀 看到 is strictly ________ ➔ 填入形容詞「mandatory」（強制的）！
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：合規遵從選 compliance，強制必須選 mandatory！
文法模組 #1：Rule 2：條件連接詞（provided that / as long as）vs. 範圍界定詞（as far as）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ provided that / providing that / as long as + S + V（只要……／在……條件下） ｜ ✅ as far as + S + know / be concerned（就……範圍而言）
  • 觀念解析：多益常將 provided that / as long as（表條件：只要……）與 as far as（表範圍程度：就……而言）放在同一組選項中。若句意為「只要滿足某項前提條件，即可執行某事」，必選 provided that 或 as long as；若句尾為 is concerned 或 I know，才選 as far as。
  • 正確示範：Full tuition reimbursement can be approved provided that both the department supervisor and human resources sign the authorization form. （只要部門主管與人資部雙方皆簽署授權表單，即可核准全額學費補助。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：中文都翻成「只要／就……」，誤用 as far as 引導條件子句。
文法模組 #2：Rule 1：疑問詞引導不定詞 (how best to) 與 介系詞關係代名詞 (介系詞 + whom / which)
  • 秒殺公式：✅ S + V + [疑問副詞 how / where / when] + best to + V ｜ ✅ 先行詞 (人), 介系詞 + whom + S + V
  • 觀念解析：多益 Part 5 兩大結構秒殺：(1) 疑問詞搭配不定詞片語作受詞（disagree over how best to advertise... 如何最妥善地宣傳），當及物動詞後已有名詞受詞時，絕不可選代名詞 what，必選副詞 how；(2) 介系詞後若接指人的先行詞，必用關係代名詞受格 whom（如 without whom, to whom, with whom），選 them 會造成 Comma Splice（逗號連接兩獨立子句）之嚴重文法錯誤，且 that 前絕不可有介系詞！
  • 正確示範：The board debated over how best to allocate the emergency contingency reserve, without which the overseas branch could not operate. （董事會激辯如何最妥善地分配緊急準備金，若沒有這筆資金，海外分行將無法運作。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 what（what to do 只有在 do 後面缺受詞時才成立；advertise 後面已有名詞受詞，必須用 how）；先行詞為人時誤選 without that（關代 that 前方嚴禁介系詞與逗號）。

SUBJECT: 2027 Academic Year Continuing Education & Professional Certification Grant

1. Annual Grant Allocation & Approved Programs
Summit Corporation is proud to foster continuous intellectual and vocational advancement across our global business teams. Under the updated Continuing Education Program, the maximum annual education subsidy per eligible employee is $3,000 per academic calendar year. Approved courses include accredited university degree modules, data science bootcamps, and professional project management certifications relevant to the employee's corporate function.

2. Grade Thresholds & Reimbursement Verification
To receive reimbursement, applicants must complete their courses with a grade of 'B' or higher (or equivalent 'Pass' in pass/fail certification exams). Official transcripts and bursar payment receipts must be transmitted to Human Resources within 30 days of course completion.

*Grant Repayment Covenant: In return for corporate educational sponsorship, recipients agree to a retention commitment. If an employee voluntarily resigns within twelve months of receiving funds, they must repay the educational grant in full via deduction from their final severance or compensation settlement.
