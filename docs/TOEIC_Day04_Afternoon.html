PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：分詞當形容詞：看被修飾名詞是「主動發出」還是「被動承受」
  • • 👀 名詞自己能做動作 ➔ 選 V-ing（demanding job 費力的工作, promising future 有前途的未來）
  • • 👀 名詞是被做的 / 承受動作 ➔ 選 V-ed（damaged goods 受損貨品, revised contract 修改後的合約）
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：主動發光選 -ing，被動受傷選 -ed！
文法模組 #1：Signals 1：be critical of + N vs. criticize + O（形容詞與及物動詞之介系詞訊號）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ be + critical + of + 名詞受詞 ｜ ✅ criticize + 名詞受詞（絕不可加 of）
  • 觀念解析：看到空格位於 be 動詞（was / were / remain）之後，且空格後方緊接介系詞 of 時，必須秒選形容詞 critical（對……持批評態度的）。反之，若空格為主要動詞且後方直接接名詞受詞（無介系詞 of），才選及物動詞 criticize。
  • 正確示範：The head of marketing was openly critical of the board's decision to reduce digital advertising expenditure. （行銷主管公開對董事會削減數位廣告支出的決定持批評態度。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 was criticized of（criticize 為及物動詞，被動語態應為 was criticized by/for，主動亦不可加 of）。
文法模組 #2：Signals 3：關係代名詞所有格陷阱（whose + N vs. of which + the + N）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ 先行詞 + whose + 無冠詞名詞 ｜ ✅ 先行詞, of which + the + 名詞
  • 觀念解析：關係代名詞所有格 whose 本身已取代所有格與冠詞，因此 whose 後方的名詞前「絕對不能有定冠詞 the」。反之，若題目空格後方的名詞前已經印出定冠詞「the + 名詞」，則絕對不可選 whose，必須改選介系詞片語「of which」（構成 of which the + N）。
  • 正確示範：Remedica, of which the key pharmaceutical products have generated enormous revenues, will expand overseas next quarter. （主打藥品創造龐大營收的 Remedica 公司將於下季拓展海外市場。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：看到所有格語意就直接選 whose，卻漏看空格後面已經印有定冠詞 the（whose the key products 為嚴重文法錯誤）。

1. Chief Executive Officer Transition
The Board of Directors of OmniMedia Holdings announces that Margaret Sterling has resolved to retire from her role as Chief Executive Officer after 12 years of transformative leadership. Under her stewardship, OmniMedia expanded from a regional print publication into an international streaming, broadcasting, and digital news conglomerate. Following an extensive international executive search, David Cho has been designated as the incoming Chief Executive Officer, assuming full operational leadership on January 15.

2. Broadcast Media Acquisition Update
Concurrently, OmniMedia Holdings provides an update regarding its proposed $450 million acquisition of Atlas Broadcast Media. The merger agreement has received unanimous approval from both corporate boards of directors and key institutional stakeholders.

*Regulatory Review Condition: Finalization of the transaction remains pending approval from the Federal Communications Commission and standard anti-trust clearance. A public shareholder town hall with David Cho and Margaret Sterling will be broadcast live on December 12 via the investor relations web portal.
