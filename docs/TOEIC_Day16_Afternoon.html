#1 subject to [形容詞片語 (phr.)] 易受...影響；須經...核准；視...而定
  • 常考搭配：The tour itinerary is subject to change without notice (行程如有變更恕不另行通知)
  • 易混淆陷阱：多益合約與通知最高頻片語！to 為介系詞，後接名詞（subject to approval / change）。
  • 實戰例句：All discounted airfare rates are subject to seat availability and seasonal peak surcharges.
    所有促銷特惠機票的折扣費率均須視當下機位訂位狀況而定，且可能隨旺季附加費用隨時變動調整。
#2 negligence [n.] 疏忽、過失、失職
  • 常考搭配：法律與品管考題高頻（如 gross negligence 嚴重過失）。 ｜ 使用者弱點語塊：gross negligence resulting in equipment malfunction (重大過失導致設備故障)

PART 2 // 本日核心文法速記（秒殺錨點 ＆ 2 大考點） 收合 / 展開
秒殺錨點：時間介系詞 (Time Sequencers) 視覺秒殺錨點
  • • 👀 看到 [________ to 名詞] 表『在……之前』➔ 秒選「Prior to」！
  • • 👀 看到 [________ to 名詞] 表『在……之後』➔ 秒選「Subsequent to」！
  • 法則：⚡ 教練金句：前選 prior to，後選 subsequent to，等待期間選 pending！
文法模組 #1：Signals 1：be critical of + N vs. criticize + O（形容詞與及物動詞之介系詞訊號）
  • 秒殺公式：✅ be + critical + of + 名詞受詞 ｜ ✅ criticize + 名詞受詞（絕不可加 of）
  • 觀念解析：看到空格位於 be 動詞（was / were / remain）之後，且空格後方緊接介系詞 of 時，必須秒選形容詞 critical（對……持批評態度的）。反之，若空格為主要動詞且後方直接接名詞受詞（無介系詞 of），才選及物動詞 criticize。
  • 正確示範：The head of marketing was openly critical of the board's decision to reduce digital advertising expenditure. （行銷主管公開對董事會削減數位廣告支出的決定持批評態度。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：誤選 was criticized of（criticize 為及物動詞，被動語態應為 was criticized by/for，主動亦不可加 of）。
文法模組 #2：Rule 2：分詞形容詞 (varied selection / informed decision) 與 否定遞進 (let alone)
  • 秒殺公式：✅ a + varied + selection / assortment + of... ｜ ✅ make + an + informed + decision ｜ ✅ 否定句 / lack + N, + let alone + 更高階事物
  • 觀念解析：多益常考「形近詞與固定搭配」盲點：(1) 修飾單數集合名詞 selection 需用分詞形容詞 varied（多樣化的），various 則直接修飾複數名詞（various options）；(2) make an informed decision 指做出深思熟慮的明智決定，informative 則是修飾演講或書籍提供豐富資訊；(3) let alone 專門置於否定或缺乏（lack / cannot）句境後，表「更不用說、何況」；(4) be opposed to + N / V-ing（to 為介系詞），主動 oppose 則直接接受詞。
  • 正確示範：Bar Italia offers a varied selection of gourmet teas, allowing clients to make an informed choice, let alone enjoying complimentary pastries. （義大利餐吧提供多樣化的頂級茶品選品，讓顧客能做出明智抉擇，更不用說還能享受免費糕點。）
  • 常見陷阱：❌ 常見陷阱：受中文影響說 a various selection（文法錯誤）；誤選 rather than 或 in contrast 替代 let alone。

In preparation for our scheduled transition to the newly constructed Metropolitan Tower on November 18, all resident departments must complete preparatory measures. Department heads and project managers must ensure that staff shred or securely lock confidential documents prior to leaving the old office premises. Unsecured sensitive client dossiers left on desks will be confiscated by internal security.

2. Employee Parking Registration & Rates
Subterranean parking permits at the new Metropolitan Tower are managed through an automated license-plate recognition system. Staff members may secure the subsidized parking rate of $60 per month by registering their license plate online before November 15 through the corporate portal.

*Non-Compliance Consequences: Employees who fail to complete their license plate registration by November 15 will forfeit the corporate subsidy and must pay the full rate of $180 per month. Visitor spaces are reserved strictly for registered external clients and auditors.
