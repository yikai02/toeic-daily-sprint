🎯 連介秒殺 ｜ 監督 vs. 疏忽 ｜ 資格與授權語塊
📦 今日核心語塊 (3-Word Chunks)
#1 oversee the project
監督這項專案
💼 商務微情境：Ms. Lin was appointed to oversee the project due to her extensive management experience.
🔄 必考替換：oversee = supervise = manage
⚠️ 注意：oversight 是名詞，指『疏忽 / 遺漏』！
#2 an unfortunate oversight
一個令人遺憾的疏忽
💼 商務微情境：Due to an unfortunate oversight, the revised invoice was sent to the wrong vendor.
🔄 必考替換：oversight = mistake = omission
#3 eligible for promotion
符合升遷資格
💼 商務微情境：Only full-time staff who complete the leadership workshop are eligible for promotion.
🔄 必考替換：eligible for = qualified for
⚠️ 必考介系詞：eligible 後方固定搭配 for + 名詞！
#4 entitled to a refund
有權獲得退款
💼 商務微情境：Customers receiving damaged items are entitled to a refund within 14 business days.
🔄 必考替換：entitled to = have the right to
⚠️ 必考介系詞：be entitled to + 名詞 / 原形動詞
#5 exempted from the fee
免除此筆費用
💼 商務微情境：Attendees registering before Friday are automatically exempted from the fee.
🔄 必考替換：exempted from = excused from
⚠️ 必考介系詞：exempted 後方固定搭配 from！
#6 compatible with the system
與該系統相容
💼 商務微情境：Please verify that the software update is compatible with the operating system.
🔄 必考替換：compatible with = adaptable to = work with
⚠️ 必考介系詞：compatible 固定搭配 with！
#7 under the direction of
在……指導／領導之下
💼 商務微情境：The new research lab was constructed under the direction of Dr. Morales.
🔄 必考替換：under the direction of = under the leadership of
🎯 視覺秒殺錨點
⚡ 無公式視覺錨點

看到空格後有沒有「動詞」？

👀 空格後有【主詞 + 動詞】➔ 秒選手拉手的「連接詞」（although, because, even if）

👀 空格後只有【名詞 / V-ing】➔ 秒選單兵作戰的「介系詞」（in spite of, despite, owing to, prior to）

⚡ 教練金句：有動詞選「連」，沒動詞選「介」！
⚡ Part 5 實戰校正題 (共 20 題)
Question 1 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
________ the unexpected budget cuts, the marketing team managed to launch the product on schedule.
(A) Although
(B) In spite of
(C) Even if
(D) Nevertheless
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q1 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：視覺錨點：空格後為 'the unexpected budget cuts'（純名詞片語無動詞），秒選介系詞。Although 與 Even if 後須接完整 S+V 子句；Nevertheless 為轉折副詞，不可放句首直接接名詞。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：儘管預算遭到意外縮減，行銷團隊仍成功如期推出該產品。
Question 2 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
The board has appointed Ms. Alvarez to ________ the newly established division in Tokyo.
(A) oversee
(B) oversight
(C) overseeing
(D) overseen
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q2 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(A)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：看到不定詞結構 'to + [原形動詞]' 帶出受詞 'the newly established division'。oversee 為動詞（監督/負責）。oversight 為名詞（疏忽），語法與語意皆不合。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：董事會已指派 Alvarez 女士負責監督東京新成立的部門。
Question 3 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Only employees who have served for over two consecutive years are ________ for the corporate leadership program.
(A) capable
(B) eligible
(C) accustomed
(D) pertinent
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q3 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：介系詞搭配秒殺：看到空格後的 'for'，直接聯想語塊 'be eligible for'（符合……資格）。capable 搭配 of；accustomed 搭配 to；pertinent 搭配 to。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：只有連續任職超過兩年的員工，才符合參加企業領導培訓計畫的資格。
Question 4 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Customers receiving damaged merchandise are ________ to a full monetary refund within thirty days.
(A) entitle
(B) entitled
(C) entitlement
(D) entitling
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q4 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：固定片語結構秒殺：be entitled to + 名詞（有權獲得……）。are 為 be 動詞，後接過去分詞形容詞 entitled。entitlement 為名詞。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：收到瑕疵受損商品的顧客有權在 30 天內獲得全額退款。
Question 5 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Non-profit educational institutions are automatically ________ from state property taxes.

Question 6 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Before installing the firmware update, please verify that it is fully ________ with your current operating system.
(A) compatible
(B) compliant
(C) comparative

🛡️ 點擊查看 Q6 答案與解析 防雷揭曉

🇹🇼 全句中譯：在安裝韌體更新之前，請確認其與您目前的作業系統完全相容。
Question 7 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
The newly established oncology laboratory was constructed under the direct ________ of Dr. Morales.
(A) direct
(B) director
(C) direction
(D) directed
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q7 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(C)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：商務高頻片語：under the direction of + 人物（在某人的領導/指導之下）。形容詞 direct 後需接名詞 direction。director 指主任，語意不通。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：新成立的腫瘤學實驗室是在莫拉萊斯博士的直接領導下建造的。
Question 8 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
The regional vice president will ________ oversee the launch of the new logistics center in Frankfurt.
(A) personal
(B) personally
(C) personality
(D) personalize
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q8 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：視覺錨點：will ________ oversee（助動詞 will 與動詞原形 oversee 之間的夾心位置，100% 填入副詞 -ly）。故選 (B) personally（親自地）。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：區域副總裁將親自監督法蘭克福新物流中心的啟用典禮。
Question 9 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
________ severe traffic disruptions along Highway 101, several keynote speakers arrived late for the morning session.
(A) Because
(B) Owing to
(C) Although
(D) Even if
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q9 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：空格後為 'severe traffic disruptions'（名詞片語無動詞），屬於介系詞受詞，表原因選 (B) Owing to。(A) Because、(C) Although、(D) Even if 後方均需接完整的 S+V 子句。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：由於 101 號高速公路沿線嚴重的交通中斷，幾位專題演講者在上午的會議中遲到了。
Question 10 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Due to an administrative ________, the revised purchase order was sent to the wrong equipment supplier.
(A) oversee
(B) oversight
(C) oversaw
(D) overseeing
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q10 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(B)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：an administrative ________（冠詞 + 形容詞 + 名詞受詞位置）。語意為『行政上的疏失/遺漏』，名詞選 (B) oversight。(A) oversee 是動詞；(C) oversaw 為過去式動詞。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：由於行政上的疏漏，修改後的採購訂單被寄到了錯誤的設備供應商手中。
Question 11 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
The CEO approved the commercial loan ________ the terms were strictly verified by the legal department.
(A) provided that
(B) in the event of
(C) regardless
(D) in spite of
🛡️ 點擊查看 Q11 答案與解析 防雷揭曉
正解：(A)
💡 視覺秒殺詳解：空格後為 'the terms were strictly verified'（S+V 完整子句），表條件假設選從屬連接詞 (A) provided that（只要 / 在……條件下）。(B) 與 (D) 是介系詞片語，後接名詞。
🇹🇼 全句中譯：只要條款經過法律部門嚴格審查，執行長便批准了該筆商業貸款。

Question 13 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
Laboratory technicians must strictly adhere ________ safety regulations when handling flammable chemicals.

Question 14 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒

Question 19 ⏱️ 限時 15 秒
While one elevator is under maintenance, building tenants should use ________ elevators located near the north entrance.
